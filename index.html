About Us

Our Story

12+ Years of Excellence

Founded in 2012, Élite Events began as a small passionate team of two in Kochi with one simple belief — every celebration deserves to be extraordinary.

Over twelve years we have grown into one of South India's most trusted event management companies, with a team of 45 professionals, a network of 200+ luxury vendors and a portfolio of 500+ breathtaking events across weddings, corporate gatherings, cultural programmes and private parties.

Our philosophy is simple: listen deeply, design boldly, execute flawlessly. We pour our hearts into understanding each client's vision and then channel our collective expertise to bring that vision to life — on time, within budget, and beyond expectation.

We require a 25% advance to confirm the booking, 50% one month before the event, and the balance on the event day. For cancellations made more than 60 days before the event, 75% of the advance is refunded. Cancellations within 30 days are non-refundable. We always recommend event insurance for peace of mind.

Yes! We manage both indoor and outdoor events extensively. For outdoor events, we arrange weather-contingency marquees, appropriate flooring, generator backup, lighting rigs and all necessary permissions from local authorities.

Yes, we operate pan-India. We have coordinated destination weddings and corporate events in Goa, Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Udaipur and Dubai. Travel and logistics costs are added separately for outstation events.

Our on-site team size depends on the scale of the event. For small events (up to 100 guests) we typically have 5–8 staff; for large events (500+ guests) we deploy 20–30 specialists including an event director, coordinators, décor team, AV technicians and hospitality staff.
